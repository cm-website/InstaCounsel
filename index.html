App Description

InstaCounsel is a revolutionary mobile application designed to democratize access to legal assistance. By leveraging cutting-edge AI technology, InstaCounsel offers a conversational AI Lawyer Bot that provides instant legal advice, making legal assistance more affordable and accessible. The app also features a Legal News aggregator, keeping users informed with the latest developments in the legal world. Additionally, the Case File Explorer allows users to access past case information, providing valuable insights and resources. InstaCounsel is committed to empowering individuals with the tools they need to navigate the legal landscape with confidence and ease.
